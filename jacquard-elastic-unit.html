01

Key Features of Jacquard Elastic:

01

Decorative Design

Features intricate woven patterns (stripes, logos, floral motifs, etc.) using the Jacquard weaving technique, which allows for complex, multi-colored designs.

02

Elasticity

Made with a mix of elastic fibers (like spandex or rubber) and non-elastic yarns (polyester, cotton, or nylon) to provide stretch and recovery.

03

Durability

High-quality Jacquard elastic retains its stretch and design even after repeated washing.

04

Soft & Comfortable

Often used in garments that touch the skin (underwear, lingerie, waistbands).

02

Common Uses:

01

Clothing

Waistbands (pants, skirts), lingerie, swimwear, socks, and sportswear.

02

Accessories

Headbands, hair ties, and decorative straps.

03

Medical & Orthopedic

Compression garments, braces (due to its comfort and adjustability).

04

Home Textiles

Elasticated fitted sheets, cushion covers.

03

Materials Used:

Elastic Core: Spandex (Lycra®), rubber, or latex for stretch.

Outer Weave: Polyester, cotton, nylon, or a blend for durability and print retention.

04

Types of Jacquard Elastic:

01

Fold-Over Elastic (FOE)

Thin, soft, and often used for lingerie edges.

02

Picot Edge Elastic

Has a decorative scalloped edge, common in bras and underwear.

03

Non-Roll Elastic

Prevents twisting, ideal for waistbands.

04

High-Stretch Jacquard Elastic

Used in activewear for maximum flexibility.

05

Advantages Over Regular Elastic:

Aesthetic Appeal – Adds a premium, branded, or fashionable look.

Customizable – Logos or unique patterns can be woven in.

Comfort – Softer than plain elastic, reducing skin irritation.
